Please find the adjacent git-format-patch attachment for mfenc.c. It is
based on FFmpeg master 359c82515228774eb683dffce9d623b6b4a2fa53,
fetched on 2026-11-22. The patch changes only the encoder property's
VARIANT type; Microsoft documents VT_BOOL for encoders, whereas VT_UI4
is the decoder-specific exception:
https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/medfound/codecapi-avlowlatencymode

The Windows ARM64 OS H.264 software MFT otherwise opens and encodes but
buffers ten frames under AV_CODEC_FLAG_LOW_DELAY. With VARIANT_TRUE
passed as VT_BOOL, metadesk's unchanged encode/decode test passes its
four-frame maximum pending-tag bound, including geometry changes and
independent reconnect decoders. The packaged minimal LGPL n9.0.2 profile
is tested separately from this rebased master patch; no full master build
or positive NVENC/AMF/QSV hardware qualification is claimed.

The one-line downstream fix is carried in metadesk until an upstream
release includes it. Nothing has been submitted by the preparation tool.
